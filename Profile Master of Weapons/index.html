Core Documents

1. Injury / Wear Drop (%)

This input measures how much a fighter’s overall performance is reduced due to accumulated damage, past injuries, long layoffs, or age-related decline.

  • 3–7% → Very fresh + active + durable
  • 8–12% → Normal wear & tear
  • 15–25% → Recent injuries or long layoff
  • 25–40%+ → Significant damage history + age

Higher % = bigger performance drop.

2. Matching Engine – Core Philosophy

Traditional models ask “Who is the better striker?” We ask a different question:

Can the fighter still access their win conditions when the fight becomes hard?

The engine models drop-off curves, not historical peaks. It combines weapon access with Fight IQ, Chaos resilience, and Injury/Wear vectors.

3. Scouting Cheat Sheet – Key Multipliers

Fight IQ Layer

  • Normal Access → 1.00
  • Poor Discipline → 0.90

Chaos / War Factor

  • Thrives in Chaos → +10%
  • Neutral → 1.00
  • Collapses under Chaos → –15%
