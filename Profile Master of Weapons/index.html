Why this exists

Fighters do not win on rankings or highlight reels. They win by accessing their weapons under pressure, fatigue, and chaos. Master of Weapons exists to measure that reality — not peak potential on a good night.

How it works

Every fighter is read through Timing, Adaptability, and Energy first. Those layers are then tested against Fight IQ, wear, and chaos resilience. The question is simple: can they still run their win conditions when the fight becomes hard?

Core Documents

1. Wear is not a footnote

Fighters are not the highlight they posted eighteen months ago.

Accumulated damage, old injuries, long layoffs, and age do not sit beside the performance. They are the performance once the fight stops being clean. Two men can share the same peak stats. Only one can still get to those stats when the body is taxed and the script is gone.

We do not grade freshness as a vibe. We treat wear as a live constraint on weapon access.

2. Matching Engine — the only question that pays

Traditional models ask: who is the better striker?

We ask: can this fighter still access their win conditions when the fight becomes hard?

The engine does not chase historical peaks. It models drop-off. Timing, adaptability, and energy first — then Fight IQ, wear, and chaos resilience on top. The output is operational access, not brochure potential.

Position is not the finish. Rankings are not the night. The cage pays for what is still available under pressure.

3. How we scout

We do not publish a multiplier sheet. The edge is in how the layers combine on a specific matchup — not in a public cheat code.

What we look at, in plain language:

  • Fight IQ — does the fighter adapt when the first weapon fails, or do they repeat the same entry until it dies?
  • Chaos — some men get sharper when the fight breaks. Some fold the moment it stops being technical.
  • Wear — what is left after the damage already taken, not what was left on their best night.

Scouting reports, division rankings, and the matching engine live behind the work. This page is the philosophy. The product is the application.
